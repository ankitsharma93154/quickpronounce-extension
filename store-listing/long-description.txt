Pronounce any word while you read.

Select a word on any page and a small card shows you:
- IPA transcription (US and UK)
- a plain-English respelling, like "pruh-NUN-see-AY-shun"
- the syllable breakdown with stress marked
- US and UK pronunciation audio
- a short definition
- a link to the full entry on quickpronounce.site

THREE WAYS TO USE IT
- Select a word, then click the "Pronounce" button that appears.
- Right-click a selection and choose "Pronounce with QuickPronounce".
- Set a keyboard shortcut and pronounce the current selection instantly.

The toolbar popup lets you type a word to look up directly, see your last
10 lookups, and check how many lookups you have left today.

PRIVACY
QuickPronounce only ever sends the single word you explicitly ask about.
It does not read page content, does not track the pages you visit, does not
build a browsing history, and includes no third-party analytics or tracking
scripts. Your recent words and settings are stored locally in your browser.

FREE
This is a free MVP. Up to 40 new words per day; words you have already looked
up keep working. Audio is generated on demand and cached so replaying a word
is instant.
