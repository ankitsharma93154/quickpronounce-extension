# Store screenshots

Chrome Web Store and Edge Add-ons want 1280x800 (or 640x400) PNG/JPEG.
Capture these five, in this order, and drop them in this folder as
`01-*.png` ... `05-*.png`.

1. **01-selection-card.png** - A real article open, a word selected, the
   "Pronounce" pill showing, and the pronunciation card open next to it.
2. **02-context-menu.png** - The right-click menu open on a selection with
   "Pronounce with QuickPronounce" highlighted.
3. **03-popup.png** - The toolbar popup: a result card shown, recent words
   list below, "12 / 40 words today" visible.
4. **04-dark-page.png** - The card on a dark-themed page, to show it stays
   readable.
5. **05-options.png** - The settings page.

Keep the chrome/browser frame minimal. Use a neutral demo page (a Wikipedia
article works well). Pick a word with a distinctive respelling, e.g.
"pronunciation", "colonel", or "entrepreneur".

A promo tile (440x280) can reuse `icons/source-logo.png` (the QuickPronounce
mark) on a light ground with the wordmark.
